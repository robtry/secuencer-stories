Well today I found two issues:

1. My architecture about 2 signaturees has a problem and it is that the failpoint is in the auth, if anybody can pass the auth, the 2 signatures are not a problem, and the attacker can do whatever he wants with the paymaster, so the auth is the most important part of the system, and I need to make sure that is really secure, and also have a good monitoring and alarms to detect any attack or issue.

And paymaster rules will change:

1. Limit txs per day per account.
1. whitelisted tokens
1. validate the precence of the `order_id` and that hasn't been used before.
1. paymaster will have a maximun budget to loss per day.
1. instead of bps will need a exchange rate from usd to the chain cost. So the fee will be `2 * exchange_rate`
1. soft limit of 5 txs per user per day, if there is a high demand, users could have less sponsored txs.
1. increase the limit of remaining balance in the accounts to be elegible for sponsorship.
